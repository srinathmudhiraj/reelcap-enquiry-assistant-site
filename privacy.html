Privacy policy

Draft for operator review · 23 November 2026

This policy describes the current private prototype of ReelCap Enquiry Assistant, a project for assisting event and decoration businesses with enquiries. The project operator can be contacted at arjunsrinath3@gmail.com.

Current scope

The UK enquiry workflow currently uses fictional test conversations and prepares drafts for review. It is not connected to a public UK customer inbox. The website has no account registration, booking form, payment collection, analytics scripts or advertising cookies.

Information used by the prototype

Test records can include an enquiry’s text, occasion, date, location, preferences, draft response, review reason and processing status. Operational records include workflow names, failed steps, error messages, execution references and notification outcomes. If you email the project, your email address and message are used to respond.

Google account and Gmail data

The current event prototype uses Gmail to send operational failure notifications to an address selected by the operator. This involves the authorised sending account, recipient address, email subject and body, and the message identifier returned by Google.

Google authorisation is completed directly with Google. Access and refresh tokens are held in the automation platform’s credential store, rather than in these website files.

The Gmail connection is configured to request send-only permission (gmail.send). The operator completed Google reconnection on 23 November 2026 and n8n shows the account connected. A single diagnostic email was accepted by Gmail after the change. Review of the resulting Google grant remains outstanding before publication. An older Google grant may still include broader access until it is removed or replaced; changing the requested scope alone does not prove that existing access has been reduced. The four workflows currently sharing this Gmail connection use it for sending email. Other Google connections, such as Sheets, need a separate review of their permissions and data uses. This policy does not authorise additional mailbox reading, modification or deletion.

Why information is processed

Information is used to organise the relevant enquiry, prepare an approved answer or human review, operate notifications and investigate faults. Commercial decisions, payment verification and booking confirmation remain human-controlled.

Systems and access

The current prototype uses an operator-managed n8n installation, Supabase for enquiry records, OpenAI for interpreting supplied test enquiry text, and Google for email notifications. WhatsApp notifications use the configured Meta connection. The current Gmail alert connection does not feed mailbox content to the enquiry model.

Authorised operators review records for the service’s operation and troubleshooting. Access should be restricted to the people responsible for the workflow. Google user data is not used for advertising, sold, or used to train general-purpose AI models. Any use of Google API data must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Test enquiry records and execution logs are retained in the configured systems until deleted under their settings or by the operator. A fixed pilot retention period has not yet been agreed. Before handling real customer enquiries, the operator must define retention and deletion procedures and update this policy.

To request access, correction or deletion of information relating to you, contact the project operator. The operator will review the request and verify the requester’s identity where needed. You can also remove the app’s Google access through your Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Revoking access stops future authorised access; it does not by itself delete previously saved records.

Before public operation

Before this website or service is launched, this draft must be checked against the actual Google permissions, all workflows using the OAuth project, the operator’s identity, the chosen website host and its request logs, retention settings and any real customer data flows. The policy must then be updated to describe the deployment accurately.
